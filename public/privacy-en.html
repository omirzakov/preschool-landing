Privacy Policy

Balam mobile app and the moyrebenok.com.kz web service · Last updated 30 July 2026

Balam is an app for preschools and schools: attendance, timetables, grades, homework, news, invoice payments and communication with teachers. Accounts are created by the educational institution — there is no self sign-up in the app.

We show no advertising, never sell or share data for marketing purposes, and use no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. The role of the educational institution

Data about students, parents and staff is entered into the system by the educational institution itself (the preschool or school). It decides which records to keep, who gets access and for how long. We process that data on the institution's behalf and only to the extent required to operate the service.

Requests to correct or delete a student's records are therefore resolved fastest directly with the institution's administration. If that is not possible, contact us and we will help.

4. Device permissions

The app requests a permission only at the moment it is needed for a specific action. Declining does not block sign-in — only the related feature becomes unavailable.

Photos and media library

Required so you can pick an image from your gallery: a profile photo, a news attachment, a document or a payment receipt. The app receives only the file you select and does not browse your library.

Location

Used by staff when checking in and out, and by administrators when marking the institution's premises on a map. Location is requested only while the app is open and in use; background access and continuous tracking are not used. Parents do not need this permission.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell data. Sharing happens only in these cases:

  • Within the educational institution — with staff, limited to their role: a teacher sees their own students, the administration sees attendance and invoices, the finance officer sees payments. A parent sees only their own child's data.
  • Hosting provider — the servers that run the service and its database (api.moyrebenok.com.kz). Data is transmitted encrypted and used solely to operate the service.
  • Google (Maps) — Google Maps is used to display the map when marking the institution's premises. Google receives the map request; no student or payment data is sent to it.
  • External apps you launch yourself — tapping the "contact teacher" button opens WhatsApp or a browser with the phone number pre-filled. Any further processing is governed by those services' own policies.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — distribution of the app and its updates. We do not send them user data.
  • Government authorities — only upon a lawful request, in the manner prescribed by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

6. What we never do

  • No advertising, and no data shared with ad networks.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking SDKs or advertising identifiers.
  • No advertising profiles and no automated decisions with legal effect.
  • No audio or video recording, and no access to your contacts or personal messages.

7. Storage and security

  • All traffic between the app and the server uses HTTPS (TLS) only.
  • Access tokens are kept in the operating system's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore), never in plain text.
  • Passwords are stored on the server as irreversible hashes.
  • Access is segregated by role: each user sees only what belongs to their function and their institution.
  • Changes to significant records are written to an audit log, so it is always possible to establish who made an edit and when.

8. Retention periods

Data is retained while the educational institution uses the service and for as long as it is needed for the purposes above. The institution can change a student's or staff member's status, or delete a record, at any time. Financial documents may be kept longer — for the period required by the accounting legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

Once an institution stops using the service, its data is deleted or anonymised within no more than 90 days, unless the law requires otherwise.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • learn what data about you we process;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • have data blocked or deleted if it is processed in breach of the law;
  • withdraw your consent to processing;
  • lodge a complaint with the authorised personal data protection authority of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

Send your request to makpal200390@mail.ru. We reply within 15 business days. So that we can verify the request comes from you, please state the phone number the account is registered to and the name of the educational institution.

10. Account and data deletion

Accounts are created and deleted by the educational institution. To have your account and its associated data deleted, contact your preschool or school administration, or e-mail us at makpal200390@mail.ru with the subject "Account deletion", stating the account phone number and the name of the institution.

We delete the profile, photo, notification history and messages within 30 days of confirming the request. Records the institution needs for accounting and academic reporting (invoices, verified payments, attendance registers) are kept for the period required by law and deleted afterwards.

11. Children

The app is intended for adult users — parents and staff of educational institutions. Children do not register in the app themselves.

Student records are entered by the educational institution as part of the educational process; a child's legal guardian may obtain those records and request their correction or deletion by contacting the institution. Where an institution issues separate access to a student, it does so with the consent of the student's legal guardian.

12. Transfers outside Kazakhstan

Data is stored on the servers that run the service. Where the hosting provider's infrastructure is located outside the Republic of Kazakhstan, transfers are made only to countries that ensure the protection of personal data, and the safeguards described in section 7 are maintained.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy — for example when new features are released. The current version is always available at moyrebenok.com.kz/privacy-en.html, and the revision date is shown at the top of the page. We announce material changes through educational institutions or via an in-app notice.
