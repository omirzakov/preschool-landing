  • consent of the data subject (or a child’s legal guardian), given to the institution at enrolment;
  • legitimate interest — keeping the service secure and preventing abuse;
  • legal obligation — in particular retention of financial and accounting records.

4. Children’s data

Balam is not intended to be used by children directly: accounts are created only for adults — parents/legal guardians and institution staff. Information about a child is entered by the institution on the basis of the guardian’s consent. A parent sees only their own child, a teacher only their group, an administrator only their institution.

5. Who we share data with

  • Your educational institution — the data owner.
  • Our hosting provider, where the system runs (api.moyrebenok.com.kz).
  • Payment providers, where online payment is used: the payment is handled on their side and card details are never passed to us.
  • Public authorities — only upon a lawful written request.

6. Storage and security

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access tokens are kept in the device’s secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore); passwords are stored server-side only as irreversible hashes.
  • Access is role-based; administrator actions are written to an audit log.
  • Data is retained while the institution’s contract with us is in force and erased within 90 days after it ends, except records we are legally required to keep (financial reporting, for the statutory period).

You may request access to your data, its correction, restriction or erasure, withdraw your consent, and ask who it has been shared with. Write to makpal200390@mail.ru — we reply within 30 calendar days. Account deletion is described in “Account and data deletion”.

8. App permissions

  • Photos / gallery — to attach an image to a news post, a document or a payment confirmation. Requested at the moment you pick a file.
  • Location — staff only, for check-in/check-out and for configuring the institution’s geofence. No background tracking.
  • Microphone — requested only if you record a voice attachment; nothing is recorded without an explicit action by you.

Any permission can be revoked in your OS settings; the related feature stops working while the rest of the app continues to function.

9. Governing law

Processing is carried out under the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan “On Personal Data and its Protection”. For users in the EEA, the GDPR additionally applies: legal bases are listed in clause 3 and data-subject rights in clause 7, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Account and data deletion

You can ask us at any time to delete your Balam account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to makpal200390@mail.ru with the subject “Account deletion”, stating the phone number the account is registered to and the name of the educational institution.
  2. We verify the requester’s identity (by a call or message to that number).
  3. The account is deleted within 30 calendar days of verification, and you receive a confirmation email.

What is deleted

  • the account: full name, phone number, password, role, institution link;
  • your in-app settings and the session tokens stored on your device;
  • files and messages you uploaded, unless they form part of the institution’s educational records.

What is retained and why

  • A child’s educational records (attendance, grades, documents) belong to the educational institution as the data owner and remain with it. Their deletion is agreed with the institution’s administration — write to us and we will pass the request on.
